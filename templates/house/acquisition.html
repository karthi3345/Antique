Private Acquisition

Conducted
personally

Acquisitions at Volgo are conversations, not checkouts. Discover, examine, verify — then enquire, and a member of the house will respond personally.

The process

  1. I

    Discover — the collection is presented as a catalogue: number, origin, period, material.

  2. II

    Examine — study the photograph, read the inspection notes.

  3. III

    Verify — provenance, condition, and documentation are open on every page.

  4. IV

    Enquire — write to the house; a member responds, usually within one working day.

  5. V

    Discuss — the object, its records, private viewing, and delivery are arranged personally.

  6. VI

    Acquire — the object, its documentation, and its provenance record pass to you.
